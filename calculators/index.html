About this toolkit

A broad collection of free online calculators for math, fitness, cooking, life events, school, finance and everyday conversions. From the basics (percentage, temperature, BMI) to the specialized (TDEE, running pace, recipe scaling, Roman numerals). Browser-based, no sign-up, no ads.

  • Cooking & nutrition — Cooking measurement converter (cups ↔ grams ↔ ml with per-ingredient densities), recipe scaler with fraction-aware math.

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators really free?

Yes — every calculator in this category is free, no sign-up, no usage limits. No data is uploaded; every calculation runs in your browser.

Do the calculators support metric and imperial units?

Yes — BMI, cooking, running pace, gas mileage and the unit converter all support both. Temperature converter handles Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin and Rankine.

How accurate are the health calculators?

The BMI / TDEE calculators use Mifflin-St Jeor (the most accurate formula for general populations) and standard activity multipliers. They're informational; consult a doctor for medical decisions.

Can I bookmark / share my calculation?

For most tools, no — the inputs are local to your browser. The few that benefit from sharing (random number generator with a seed, for example) explicitly support it.
