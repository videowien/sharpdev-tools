Frequently asked questions

Are these finance calculators accurate?

They use standard formulas (compound interest, mortgage amortization, ROI annualization). For tax decisions consult a qualified accountant; for investment decisions consult a financial advisor — these are educational tools, not advice.

Do they handle different currencies?

Yes — most tools are currency-agnostic (you pick the symbol). Live exchange rates are deliberately not used (would require a backend) so calculations are deterministic and offline-friendly.

Is my financial data uploaded?

No — everything runs in your browser. Net Worth Tracker stores entries in your browser's localStorage only.

How accurate is the mortgage amortization?

It uses the standard amortization formula. Real-life mortgages may differ slightly due to fees, escrow, prepayment options — check with your lender for the exact schedule.
