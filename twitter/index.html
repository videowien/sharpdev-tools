Frequently asked questions

How long can a tweet be?

280 characters for free X users. Premium / X Premium users get 4000. The character counter shows both limits and warns when you cross either.

Does the thread splitter add numbering automatically?

Yes — splits to fit the 280 limit and adds 1/n, 2/n, … numbering. You can edit each part before posting.

What's the right size for an X / Twitter banner?

1500×500 pixels. The Banner Sizer shows you the safe zones where your profile picture overlaps so you don't lose important content.

Are these Twitter tools free?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, no tracking, no Twitter login required.
