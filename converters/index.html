About this toolkit

A growing set of single-purpose unit converters. Each one does exactly one conversion (cm to inches, kg to lbs, Celsius to Fahrenheit) with a live result and a quick-reference table, so you land, read the answer, and leave. No multi-unit menus to navigate.

  • Everyday measurements — Convert metric and imperial length, weight and volume: cm to inches, kg to lbs, ml to cups, the conversions you actually look up.
  • Cooking & recipes — Switch recipe amounts between ml, cups, tablespoons and teaspoons without a multi-step calculator.
  • Travel & driving — Speed conversions like mph to km/h, and distances like km to miles, at a glance.
  • Tech & storage — Data-size conversions (MB to GB, KB to MB) in decimal SI units.

Frequently asked questions

Why one converter per page instead of one big tool?

Because that is how people search: someone types "cm to inches", not "open a unit converter and pick units". A dedicated page gives the answer instantly with no menu, plus a reference table for common values.

Are the conversions exact?

They use the official conversion factors (for example 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly) and display several significant figures. Rounding only affects the last shown digit.

Do the data-size converters use 1000 or 1024?

They use decimal SI units (1 GB = 1000 MB), which is how storage is marketed and how most people mean it. Binary units (1 GiB = 1024 MiB) differ; the relevant tool notes this.

Is there a multi-unit converter too?

Yes, the Unit Converter in Calculators handles many units at once. These single converters are the fast path for one specific, common conversion.
