About this toolkit

A set of browser and device tests that run entirely in your browser: check your webcam and microphone, test a game controller, hunt for dead pixels, measure your click speed and reaction time, read your screen resolution and User-Agent, and test multi-touch. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Who uses these tools

  • Before a video call — Confirm your webcam shows a picture and your microphone picks up sound in seconds, before joining a meeting or interview.
  • Hardware checks — Test every key on a keyboard, find dead or stuck pixels on a monitor, and check a game controller's buttons and sticks all register.
  • Gamers — Measure clicks per second and reaction time, and verify a gamepad's buttons, triggers and analogue sticks before a match.
  • Developers & support — Read the exact screen resolution, pixel ratio and User-Agent string, and check multi-touch behaviour when debugging a device.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tests private?

Yes. Every test runs locally in your browser. The webcam and microphone tests never record or upload anything; the camera and mic only turn on after you allow access and stop when you leave the page.

Why does the webcam or mic test ask for permission?

Browsers require your explicit consent before any site can use the camera or microphone. The test only starts after you click Start and approve, which protects your privacy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every tool works in a normal browser with no downloads, plugins or sign-up. A game controller just needs to be plugged in or paired, then press a button to wake it.

Which tools are in this category?

Webcam test, microphone test, gamepad tester, multi-touch test, keyboard tester, dead pixel test, screen resolution info, User-Agent viewer, click speed (CPS) test and reaction time test.
