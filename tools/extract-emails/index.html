Extract Email Addresses

Find and list every email address in a block of text. Dedupe and sort optional.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract emails from a block of text?

Paste the text and every valid-looking email address is pulled out and listed one per line. It works on logs, exports, pasted documents or any messy text.

Can it remove duplicates?

Yes, duplicate removal is on by default (case-insensitively), and you can also sort the results alphabetically.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Extraction happens in your browser, so even sensitive contact lists stay on your device.
