Frequently asked questions

How is the ovulation date estimated?

It assumes ovulation happens about 14 days before your next period starts. The next period is your last period plus your cycle length, so a longer cycle pushes ovulation later. This is the standard calendar estimate.

What is the fertile window?

It is the days when conception is most likely: the five days leading up to ovulation plus ovulation day itself. Sperm can survive several days, which is why the window starts before ovulation.

Can I rely on this for birth control?

No. Calendar estimates are approximate and cycles vary month to month, so this tool must not be used as contraception or to diagnose pregnancy. For those, use a medical method or speak to a healthcare professional.
