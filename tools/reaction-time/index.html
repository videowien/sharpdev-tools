Frequently asked questions

How does the reaction time test work?

Click to start, then wait. The box turns red, and after a random delay it turns green. The moment it turns green, click as fast as you can. The tool measures the milliseconds between the green and your click.

What is a good reaction time?

A typical visual reaction time is about 200 to 300 milliseconds. Anything below 200 ms is fast, and around 150 ms is near the human limit for reacting to a visual cue.

Why did it say 'Too soon'?

You clicked while the box was still red, before it turned green. Wait for the colour change, then click.

Does it store my results?

No. Your tries are kept only on the page while it is open and are never uploaded.
