Why the counts differ between platforms

Each platform sets its own limit and counts characters slightly differently:

  • Twitter / X — 280 chars, but URLs always count as 23, and some non-Latin characters count as 2
  • Instagram — 2200 chars for captions, including hashtags and mentions
  • LinkedIn — 3000 chars for posts, but only the first 210 show above "see more"
  • TikTok — 2200 chars for descriptions (was 150 before 2023)
  • Threads — 500 chars
  • Bluesky — 300 chars (300 graphemes)
  • Mastodon — 500 default, but many instances allow up to 11000
  • Facebook — 63,206 (effectively no limit)
  • Pinterest — 500 char description

Privacy

All counting happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
