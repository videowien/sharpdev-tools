Frequently asked questions

How do I count how many times a word appears?

Type the word, phrase or character in the search box and paste your text below. The count updates live, along with the total word count and what percentage of words your term represents.

What does 'whole word' do?

With it on, searching 'cat' matches only the standalone word 'cat', not 'category' or 'concatenate'. With it off, every substring match is counted.

Is it case-sensitive?

By default no. Untick 'ignore case' to count only exact-case matches.
