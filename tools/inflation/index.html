Forward vs backward

  • Today → future — how much you'll need then to have the same purchasing power as today. Useful for retirement, sabbatical, or long-term savings planning.
  • Past → today — what an old price would be in today's money. Useful for historical comparisons ("dad's first house cost $40k in 1985 — what's that today?").

The compound effect

People underestimate compound inflation. At 3% for 30 years, prices roughly 2.4×. At 5% for 30 years, prices 4.3×. Salary plans, retirement targets, and education savings goals should all be adjusted for at least one full inflation cycle.
