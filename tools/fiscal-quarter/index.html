Frequently asked questions

How are quarters defined?

A quarter is a three-month block of the year. For the calendar year Q1 is January to March, Q2 April to June, Q3 July to September and Q4 October to December. This tool shows which one any date falls in.

What is a fiscal quarter?

Many organisations run a financial year that does not start in January. If yours starts in, say, April, then April to June is fiscal Q1. Pick your fiscal year start and the quarters shift accordingly.

What do the day-of and days-remaining figures mean?

They show how far through the current quarter your date is and how many days are left in it, which is useful for tracking targets and deadlines.
