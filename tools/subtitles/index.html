YouTube Subtitle Downloader

Paste YouTube links and download auto-generated subtitles as a ZIP file.

Limitations & how it works
  • Subtitle requests are made from your browser through our proxy server.
  • YouTube may temporarily block requests if too many are made too quickly.
  • To stay safe, this tool processes 15 links at a time with a 5-second delay between each, and waits 10 minutes between batches.
  • Only auto-generated or manually uploaded subtitles are downloaded — not the video itself.
  • Some videos may not have subtitles available in your chosen language.
  • If downloads fail, wait ~30 minutes and try again.
