Frequently asked questions

How do I blur an image?

Choose a photo, then drag the blur radius slider until you like the effect. A higher radius gives a stronger, softer blur. Click Download PNG to save it.

Can I blur just part of the image?

This tool blurs the whole image. For hiding a face or a detail, blur the image and then crop or overlay it, or use a dedicated redaction tool.

Is it private?

Yes. The blur is applied locally in your browser with a canvas filter, so the photo is never uploaded.
