Frequently asked questions

Is my microphone audio recorded?

No. The sound is analysed in real time only to drive the level meter, and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. Everything happens locally in your browser.

The meter does not move when I speak, why?

Your system may be using a different microphone, the input may be muted, or access was blocked. Check your operating system's sound input settings and the microphone permission in the browser's address bar.

Does it work for testing a headset or USB mic?

Yes. It tests whichever microphone your browser uses by default. Select the device you want as the default input in your system settings, then start the test.
