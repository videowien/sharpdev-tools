Frequently asked questions

How do I read a QR code?

Drop or choose an image of a QR code (a photo or screenshot works), or click Use camera and point it at one. The text or link inside is decoded and shown instantly. Everything happens in your browser.

Does it open links automatically?

No. For safety a decoded link is shown as plain text and never opened on its own, so you can check it first. QR codes can hide malicious links, so always read the address before visiting.

Is my image or camera uploaded?

No. Decoding runs entirely in your browser with a local library, so the image and the camera feed never leave your device and nothing is sent to a server.
