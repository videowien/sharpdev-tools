Extract URLs

Find and list every link in a block of text. Dedupe and sort optional.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a URL?

Anything starting with http://, https:// or www. is matched. Trailing punctuation like a period or comma at the end of a sentence is trimmed off.

Can it dedupe and sort?

Yes, duplicate removal is on by default and you can sort the list alphabetically.

Does it run locally?

Yes, entirely in your browser, nothing is uploaded.
