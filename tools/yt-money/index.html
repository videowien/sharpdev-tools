Why niche matters more than view count

A finance channel with 50k views/month can out-earn a gaming channel with 500k views/month — because advertisers pay 5-10× more to reach finance viewers (high purchase intent, B2B audiences, recurring software subscriptions to sell).

What this doesn't include

The estimate covers AdSense revenue only. Real income for established channels usually includes: brand sponsorships (often more than AdSense), affiliate links, channel memberships, merchandise, paid courses, Patreon. Many creators in the 10k-100k subscribe range earn 3-10× their AdSense from sponsorships alone.
