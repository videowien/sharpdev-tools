Frequently asked questions

How do I find a song's BPM?

Play the song and tap the button (or any key) in time with the beat. After a few taps the tool shows the tempo in beats per minute, both precise and rounded to the nearest whole number.

Why does the reading change as I tap?

It averages the time between your recent taps, so each tap refines the estimate. Keep a steady rhythm for four or five taps and it settles. Uneven tapping makes it wobble.

What counts as a typical tempo?

Most pop and rock sits around 100 to 130 BPM, ballads slower, dance and techno often 120 to 140. Tapping on the half or double beat will read half or twice the real tempo, so tap the main pulse.
