How does image to PDF conversion work?

This tool uses jsPDF to build a fresh PDF in your browser. Each image you upload becomes one page. You control the order (drag the cards), the page size (A4, Letter, or auto-fit to image), the orientation, and the margin around each image. Nothing leaves your device — even sensitive scans like ID cards or signed contracts stay local.

Combine multiple JPGs into one PDF

Drop multiple JPGs at once. Drag the thumbnails to reorder. Click × on any thumbnail to remove that image before creating the final PDF. You can also drop additional images later — they get appended to the end of the list.

Which page size should I pick?

  • A4 — European standard, default for most printers and digital documents.
  • Letter — US standard, slightly wider and shorter than A4.
  • Auto · match image — each page exactly matches its image's dimensions. Useful for photo books or screenshots where you don't want whitespace around the image.
