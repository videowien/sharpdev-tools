Frequently asked questions

How is the required final score calculated?

Your current grade counts for everything except the final, and the final counts for its weight. The tool solves for the final score that makes the weighted total equal your target: (target − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight.

What if it says I need more than 100%?

Then your target is out of reach with the final alone, even with a perfect paper. Either set a lower target, or double-check the final's weight and your current grade.

What does a negative or zero requirement mean?

It means you have already done enough. Even scoring zero on the final would leave you at or above your target, so the requirement is effectively nothing.
