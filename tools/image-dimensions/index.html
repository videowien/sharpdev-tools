Frequently asked questions

How do I check an image's dimensions?

Drop the image onto the box or click to choose one. Its width and height in pixels appear immediately, along with the aspect ratio, megapixels, file size, format and orientation.

What is the aspect ratio?

It is the width-to-height proportion in simplest whole numbers, like 16:9 or 4:3. It is useful for checking whether an image fits a layout, a video frame or a print size without distortion.

Is my image uploaded to check it?

No. The image is read locally in your browser to measure it, and nothing is sent to any server.
