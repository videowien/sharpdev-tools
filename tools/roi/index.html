What's a "good" ROI?

  • S&P 500 long-term average — ~10% annualised
  • Real estate — 8-12% annualised including rental yield
  • High-yield savings — 4-5% (currently, late-2025)
  • Business / startup — VCs target 25-40% annualised on portfolio; failures included
  • Inflation — 2-3% baseline; any return below this is a loss in real terms

ROI vs annualised ROI — why both matter

A 50% ROI over 10 years = 4.1% annualised — slower than the S&P. A 30% ROI over 2 years = 14% annualised — way better. Always check both numbers before comparing investments of different durations.
