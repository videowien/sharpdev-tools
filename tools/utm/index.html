Good naming conventions

  • Lowercase everything — GA treats Facebook and facebook as different sources, splitting your data unintentionally.
  • Underscores, not spaces — spaces get URL-encoded to %20 which looks ugly in reports.
  • Consistent vocabulary — don't use fb on one campaign and facebook on another. Pick one and stick with it across the org.
  • Date in campaign name — summer_sale_2026 beats summer_sale because you'll re-use the name in 2027 and not be able to compare year-over-year.

Privacy

All URL building runs in your browser. The tool doesn't fetch, log, or store anything.
