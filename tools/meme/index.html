Frequently asked questions

What font does it use?

The classic meme look uses Impact (with Arial Black as a fallback) in bold white with a black outline — the traditional caption style.

Why is my text in capitals?

Meme captions are traditionally uppercase, so the tool capitalizes automatically for that recognizable look.

Is the image uploaded anywhere?

No. The meme is drawn on a canvas in your browser and downloaded locally; nothing is sent to a server.
