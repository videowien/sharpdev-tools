Frequently asked questions

What does the touch test show?

Every point of contact (a finger on a touchscreen, or your mouse or trackpad pointer) appears as a circle while it is down. The counters show how many points are active at once and the most you have reached, so you can check multi-touch.

How do I test multi-touch?

On a touchscreen, place several fingers in the area at the same time. The active-points counter rises with each finger, confirming how many simultaneous touches your screen supports. Most phones handle five or ten.

Can I find dead zones on my screen?

Yes. Slowly drag a finger across the whole area. If the circle stops following in a particular spot, that part of the digitiser may not be registering touch.
