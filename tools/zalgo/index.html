Frequently asked questions

What is zalgo text?

Zalgo text stacks many Unicode ‘combining marks’ on top of normal letters, making them look corrupted or glitchy. The underlying letters are unchanged — it's purely decorative.

Will it work on Instagram, Discord and TikTok?

Yes on most platforms. A few apps strip combining marks or limit how tall text can render, so very high intensity may look clipped. Lower the intensity if it gets cut off.

Is it random?

Yes — each character gets a random set of marks, so every generation looks a little different.
