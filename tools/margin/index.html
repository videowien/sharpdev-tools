Margin vs markup — quick reference

  • Margin = profit / selling price × 100. Tells you what fraction of revenue is profit.
  • Markup = profit / cost × 100. Tells you how much you added on top of cost.

Industry benchmark margins

  • Grocery: 1–3%
  • E-commerce (retail): 5–10%
  • Restaurants: 3–9% (food cost ~30% means 70% gross margin on food, but ops eat most of it)
  • Professional services: 20–50%
  • Software / SaaS: 70–90%
  • Luxury goods: 50–80%
