Common countdown use cases

  • Product launches — "47 days until ship date" creates urgency
  • Birthdays / anniversaries — pin to a tab for awareness
  • Holidays / events — Christmas, New Year, your trip
  • Personal deadlines — exam dates, project hand-ins, end of quarter
  • Page widget — copy the URL with prefilled target as a permalink

How precise is the countdown?

The timer ticks every 250ms — accurate to within a second. The target moment is interpreted in your local timezone (whatever your computer says). If you need fixed UTC, translate first with the Time Zone Converter.

Privacy

The countdown runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
