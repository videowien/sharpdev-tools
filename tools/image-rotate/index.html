When you need rotation

  • Photos with wrong EXIF orientation — phone photos sometimes display sideways depending on the app. Re-saving fixes the orientation
  • Scanned documents — flatbed scans rarely match the desired orientation
  • Stickers / patterns — flip to make mirror-image variants for design layouts
  • Print prep — rotate landscape photos to portrait for specific layouts

Lossless vs lossy

Rotations of 90°, 180°, 270° are lossless — every original pixel is preserved, just at a new position. Arbitrary angles (1-89°) require interpolation, which slightly softens edges. The custom-angle slider uses bilinear interpolation.
