How engagement rate works

The standard public-data formula is (Likes + Comments) / Followers × 100. It's a percentage: a 3% engagement rate means 3 of every 100 followers interact with your average post (via like or comment).

Why engagement matters more than follower count

  • Algorithm signal — Instagram's feed and Explore ranking use engagement to decide which posts to surface. High-ER accounts get more organic reach per post.
  • Sponsorship value — brands often pay per-engagement, not per-follower. A 50K account with 5% ER can earn more than a 200K account with 0.8% ER.
  • Audience quality — high ER signals an active, real, interested audience. Low ER suggests followers may be inactive, bought, or off-niche.

How to improve your engagement rate

  • Post when YOUR audience is active (check Instagram Insights).
  • Use carousels — they consistently outperform single-image posts.
  • Ask questions in captions to drive comments.
  • Reply to every comment in the first hour to push the algorithm.
  • Stop buying followers — it tanks ER and is detectable.
