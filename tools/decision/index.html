The "secret" of a Yes/No generator

If a random Yes makes you immediately want to flip it back to No (or vice versa), you actually knew what you wanted — you were just stuck. The trick of these tools isn't the result, it's the moment of noticing your reaction.

When this isn't the right tool

  • Big life decisions — talk to a friend or a therapist, not a random generator
  • Decisions involving other people — they deserve more than a coin flip
  • When you're tired — go to bed first, decide tomorrow

When it's perfect

  • Trivial choices that consume disproportionate energy — what to eat, what to watch, where to walk
  • "Should I take a break?" — almost always yes anyway
  • Breaking analysis paralysis on truly even options
