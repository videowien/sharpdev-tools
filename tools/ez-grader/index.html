Frequently asked questions

How does the EZ grader work?

It divides the number of correct answers (total minus wrong) by the total number of questions and shows the percentage and a letter grade. It is the digital version of the classic teacher's grading wheel.

Can I change the grading scale?

The tool uses the common 90/80/70/60 scale for A to F. If your school uses different cut-offs, read the percentage and apply your own scale; the percentage itself does not change.

Does it handle extra-credit or weighted questions?

No, it assumes every question is worth the same. For weighted categories or extra credit, use the weighted grade calculator instead.
