The 8 moon phases

  • 🌑 New Moon — completely dark; rises with the sun
  • 🌒 Waxing Crescent — sliver visible on the right (Northern Hemisphere)
  • 🌓 First Quarter — right half lit; rises ~noon
  • 🌔 Waxing Gibbous — more than half but not full
  • 🌕 Full Moon — fully lit; rises at sunset
  • 🌖 Waning Gibbous — past full, shrinking
  • 🌗 Last Quarter — left half lit; rises ~midnight
  • 🌘 Waning Crescent — sliver on the left; rises before dawn

What the phases are useful for

  • Astrophotography — new moon = darkest skies for deep-sky shots
  • Landscape photo at night — full moon lights the scene without flash
  • Fishing — many anglers track lunar phases
  • Gardening — traditional planting calendars follow the moon
