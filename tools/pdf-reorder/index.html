Drag any thumbnail to reorder. Click ✕ to remove a page entirely.

Common use cases

  • Scanner fixed sequence wrong — multi-page scanners sometimes feed pages backwards or in groups
  • Append a cover — move the title page to the front after compiling
  • Reorder a presentation — last-minute slide rearranging after exporting from PowerPoint
  • Combine + reorder — after Merge PDF, the order is whatever you uploaded. Use this to fix it.

Privacy

The PDF is parsed and rewritten in your browser via pdf-lib + pdf.js. Nothing is uploaded.
