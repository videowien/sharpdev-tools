About Instagram Story Highlights

Highlights are saved Stories that live on your profile permanently, above your feed grid. They're an underrated branding surface — the row of circular covers right under your bio is one of the first things visitors see. A consistent visual style across covers makes a profile look intentional and on-brand.

How Instagram crops the cover

Even though you upload a 9:16 (1080×1920) image, Instagram shows it as a circle at ~78×78px in the profile view. The crop is centered, so any icon you place should be in the dead center of the canvas. This tool does that automatically — the icon is rendered centered with appropriate sizing so it fits inside the circular crop.

Design tips

  • Stick to one or two colors across all covers — that's what makes the row look like a set.
  • One icon per cover — emoji or a single letter. Anything more gets cropped or unreadable.
  • Match your overall feed — if your feed is warm/beige, beige cover backgrounds blend. If it's vibrant, contrast.
  • White on dark vs dark on light — the tool auto-picks readable icon color based on background luminance.
