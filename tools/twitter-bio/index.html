Twitter / X bio cheat sheet

  • 160 characters total — URLs count at their full length (not shortened the way they are inside tweets)
  • The first ~100 characters carry the weight — mobile previews truncate before "more"
  • Front-load value — what do you do? Why should someone follow? Cleverness can come second
  • Use an active CTA — "→ free guide" or "↓ latest project" outperforms passive descriptions

What works in 160 chars

  • Identity + one verb — "Backend dev. Writing about Rust." (32 chars)
  • Three nouns — "Builder · Writer · Coffee enjoyer · sharpdev.tools" (51 chars)
  • Question-as-bio — "What if every dev tool was free?" (33 chars) — provokes engagement
  • List with separators — "✏️ writing · 🛠 building · 🚀 shipping" (43 chars)
