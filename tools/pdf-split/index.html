How do I split a PDF online?

This tool uses pdf-lib running in your browser to copy selected pages from your source PDF into one or more new PDFs. Nothing is uploaded — confidential documents stay on your device.

Range syntax

  • 5 — only page 5
  • 1-5 — pages 1 through 5
  • 1, 3, 5 — pages 1, 3 and 5 only
  • 1-5, 8, 10-12 — combined ranges and individual pages

Range mode vs Each-page mode

  • Extract pages — produces one PDF containing the pages you specified, in the order they appear in the source.
  • Split every page — produces N single-page PDFs (one per page in the source), bundled as a ZIP for convenient download.

Common use cases

  • Extracting just the signature page from a contract
  • Sending only chapter 3 of a long report
  • Splitting a scanned multi-page document into individual files for indexing
  • Pulling out a single page to share via email or chat without exposing the rest
