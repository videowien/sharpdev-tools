Keyword Density Analyzer

Single words, 2-word phrases and 3-word phrases — sorted by frequency.

How to use the data

  • Top single words — confirm your target keyword is actually frequent (1-2%)
  • Top 2-word phrases — see what your real topic clusters are; align with search intent
  • Top 3-word phrases — surface long-tail variations you're already ranking adjacent to
  • Stopword filtering — without it the top result is always "the" / "of" / "and"

Density isn't everything

Modern SEO weights topical depth (related entities, semantic completeness) far more than exact density. Use keyword density to spot gaps and over-stuffing, not as a target to hit.
