How does page deletion work?

This tool reads your PDF with pdf.js to render thumbnails so you can see every page, then uses pdf-lib to build a new PDF containing only the pages you kept. Marked pages are simply skipped — their content isn't copied into the output. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Click thumbnails to toggle deletion

Click any page thumbnail to mark it for deletion (red overlay with ×). Click again to un-mark. The counter at the top shows "X pages will be kept, Y removed" so you know exactly what you're saving. The page order in the output matches the original — you're only removing, not reordering.

Common use cases

  • Removing a confidential signature page before forwarding a contract
  • Stripping blank pages from a scanned document
  • Deleting a cover sheet from a report before publishing
  • Removing duplicate or unwanted pages from a merged PDF
