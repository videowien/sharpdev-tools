PDF to Word (.docx)

Honest text-only conversion. Best for text-heavy documents (articles, books, reports).

⚠ Honest scope: This tool extracts text + detected headings into a clean .docx. It does NOT preserve complex layouts (multi-column, tables, image positioning). For pixel-perfect Word output you need Adobe Acrobat Pro — and even that isn't always perfect.

Drop a PDF here or click to browse



















When this works well


    

  • Articles, blog posts, papers — text-driven content with clear hierarchy
    • 

  • Books and reports — single-column, paragraph-based layout
    • 

  • Legal documents where you need editable text and don't care about original formatting
    • 




When it'll disappoint


    

  • Magazines / newspapers — multi-column flows into single-column gibberish
    • 

  • Forms with positioned fields — boxes / lines / labels lose their relationships
    • 

  • Spreadsheets exported as PDF — table structure is mostly lost; try a dedicated table extractor
    • 

  • Image-heavy designs — images become [image] placeholders
    • 




Privacy


Everything runs in your browser. pdf.js for parsing, JSZip for building the .docx file.
