Remove Line Breaks

Strip newlines from pasted text. Join with spaces, remove entirely, or keep paragraph breaks.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove line breaks from text?

Paste your text and pick a mode: 'Join with a space' turns wrapped lines into one flowing paragraph, 'Remove entirely' deletes every newline, and 'Keep paragraph breaks' collapses single line breaks but preserves blank-line paragraph separators.

When is this useful?

Cleaning text copied from PDFs or emails where every line wraps with a hard return, preparing text for a single cell, or flattening multi-line content before pasting into a form.

Does it run in my browser?

Yes, entirely client-side. Nothing is uploaded.
