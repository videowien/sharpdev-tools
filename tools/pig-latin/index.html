Frequently asked questions

What are the Pig Latin rules?

Words starting with a vowel get 'way' added (egg becomes eggway). Words starting with consonants move the leading consonant cluster to the end and add 'ay' (string becomes ingstray, pig becomes igpay).

Does it keep capitals and punctuation?

Yes. Capitalization is preserved on each word and punctuation stays in place; only the letters are transformed.
