Frequently asked questions

How is the crop chosen?

The largest centered square of your photo is taken, scaled to your chosen size, and (for circle) clipped to a circle with a transparent background.

Why a transparent PNG?

So a circular avatar has clean transparent corners and sits nicely on any background, like profile pictures and chat apps.

Can I reposition the crop?

This tool center-crops automatically. For off-center crops, pre-crop with the image crop tool first.
