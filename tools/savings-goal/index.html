The maths

Monthly compounding with regular contributions:

  • Future value — FV = P × (1 + r)n + PMT × [((1 + r)n - 1) / r]
  • P = starting balance, PMT = monthly contribution, r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12), n = months
  • Solve for PMT to find required monthly amount; solve for n to find months required.
