Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an image to grayscale?

Drop or choose a photo, and it is converted to black and white instantly. Use the intensity slider for a partial desaturation, then click Download PNG to save it.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion happens entirely in your browser using a canvas, so your image never leaves your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

What does intensity do?

At 100% the image is fully black and white. Lower values blend the grayscale with the original colours, giving a faded or muted look rather than full desaturation.
