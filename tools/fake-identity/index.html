Fake Identity Generator

Generate realistic but fake people with name, email, phone, address, company and job, for testing and mock-ups.

All data is randomly assembled and fictional. Any resemblance to a real person, address or company is coincidental. Use it for seeding databases, demos and form testing, never to deceive.

Frequently asked questions

Are these real people?

No. Every name, address, email, phone number and company is randomly combined from generic lists and is entirely fictional. The email domains (like example.com) are reserved for documentation, so they cannot receive mail.

What is fake identity data good for?

Seeding test databases, filling out demos and screenshots, checking how forms and layouts handle real-looking data, and load testing, all without exposing anyone's real personal information.

Is the address real?

The street, city and postal code are plausible-looking but randomly assembled and not intended to match any real address. Use the data only for testing, never to impersonate or deceive.
