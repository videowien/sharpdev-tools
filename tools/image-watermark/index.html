Frequently asked questions

Can I watermark a batch of images?

This tool does one image at a time. Load, position the text, download, then load the next.

Is the watermark permanent?

It's burned into the downloaded PNG. Keep your original if you might want a different watermark later.

Does the image leave my device?

No. The watermark is drawn on a canvas in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
