LinkedIn comment best practices

  • 50-150 characters wins — short comments get more replies, which feeds the algorithm
  • End with a question — invites the original poster to reply, doubling your engagement
  • Don't pitch in comments — the platform demotes comments that look like ads
  • Reply to other comments — replies inside a thread count as separate engagement signals
  • Tag with intent only — random @mentions are pruned by the spam filter

What "see more" actually means

After roughly 3 visible lines (≈200-250 chars depending on font rendering), LinkedIn truncates the comment with "…see more". Less than 5% of viewers click. Front-load the value.

Privacy
