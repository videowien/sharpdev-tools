Frequently asked questions

How do binaural beats work?

When each ear hears a slightly different pure tone (say 200 Hz and 210 Hz), the brain perceives a third, pulsing tone at the difference (10 Hz). That perceived pulse is the binaural beat. It only happens with headphones, because each ear must hear only its own tone.

What are the brainwave bands?

The presets map the beat frequency to EEG bands often associated with mental states: delta with deep sleep, theta with meditation, alpha with relaxed focus, beta with alertness and gamma with concentration. Pick a band or set the carrier and beat yourself.

Are binaural beats scientifically proven?

Evidence is mixed and effects are subtle and individual. Treat them as a relaxation or focus aid to experiment with, not a medical treatment. Keep the volume comfortable.
