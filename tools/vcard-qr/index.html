Frequently asked questions

What is a vCard QR code?

It is a QR code containing a vCard, the standard digital contact format. When scanned, the phone offers to save all the details (name, phone, email, company, website) as a new contact in one tap.

Where should I use it?

On business cards, conference badges, email signatures, slides and shop windows. It saves people from typing your details and reduces mistakes. You can also download the raw .vcf file to share directly.

Do all the fields have to be filled?

No. Only the fields you complete are included. A name plus one way to reach you (phone, email or website) is enough for a useful contact card.
