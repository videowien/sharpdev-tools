How the emoji search works

Every emoji in the grid is indexed by both its name and its common keywords. Type into the search box and the grid filters in real-time — match anywhere in the name or any keyword. Examples:

  • "heart" — surfaces ❤️ 💛 💚 💙 💜 🖤 🤍 🤎 plus 💗 💖 💘 💝
  • "fire" — surfaces 🔥 and any emoji tagged with fire/hot/lit
  • "pizza" — direct name match 🍕
  • "happy" — surfaces 😀 😃 😄 😁 😆 😊 and other smileys tagged happy
  • "thumbs up" — 👍 (multi-word name match works)
  • "love" — hearts, kisses 😘 💋, eyes 😍 🥰

Emoji search vs emoji finder vs emoji picker — same tool, different words

People searching for an "emoji finder", "emoji search", "emoji lookup", "emoji picker" or "emojifinder" all want the same thing: a fast way to locate a specific emoji and copy it. This tool does all of that — the search box covers the lookup/search/find use case, the 9 category tabs cover the browse/picker use case, and a single click copies any emoji to your clipboard.

Browse emojis by category

When you don't know the exact name, switch to category browsing. Nine categories cover the full common emoji set:

  • Smileys & Emotion — faces, hearts, gestures of feeling
  • People & Body — hand gestures, body parts, professions
  • Animals & Nature — animals, plants, weather
  • Food & Drink — meals, ingredients, beverages
  • Travel & Places — vehicles, landmarks, buildings
  • Activities — sports, hobbies, events
  • Objects — household items, tools, tech
  • Symbols — math, arrows, warnings, hearts
  • Flags — country and regional flags

Privacy & how it runs

Everything runs locally in your browser. No emoji, no click and no search query is ever sent to a server. Recently used emojis are stored in your browser's localStorage only.
