Why 1080×1080 (not 320×320)?

Instagram's profile pic displays at 320×320 in the app and 110×110 in feeds. But uploading at exactly 320×320 means Instagram re-compresses a small image, and the result is fuzzy. Uploading at 1080×1080 (or larger square) lets Instagram pick a clean down-sample, giving you crisper edges on Retina/HiDPI screens and in story-ring previews.

Composition tips

  • Center your face — Instagram displays as circle; corners get cropped
  • Tight crop on the head/shoulders — the profile pic shows tiny in feeds (~40px); details below the shoulders won't be visible
  • High-contrast background — your profile picture sits inside a story-ring border; busy backgrounds compete
  • Match your overall feed aesthetic — if your feed is muted, a fluorescent green PFP looks out of place
