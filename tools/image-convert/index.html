Which format should you use?

  • JPG / JPEG — best for photos (gradients, natural colors). Lossy. Doesn't support transparency.
  • PNG — lossless. Best for screenshots, text-heavy graphics, logos. Supports transparency. Files are larger than JPG.
  • WebP — both lossy (replaces JPG) and lossless (replaces PNG). Typically 25–35% smaller than the same image in PNG or JPG at the same visual quality. Supported by all modern browsers.
