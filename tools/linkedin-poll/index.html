LinkedIn poll best practices

  • 1-week duration wins — long enough for your whole network to see, short enough to feel current
  • Open with "What's your take" or "Which would you pick" — invites a decision
  • Add "Curious to hear in comments" in the post body — polls + comments together boost reach
  • 2 options outperform 4 — same as Twitter, decisive framings get more votes
  • Avoid leading questions — "Why should I avoid the bus?" reads as biased

Why polls boost reach on LinkedIn

LinkedIn weighs dwell time + vote action as high-value engagement. A poll typically gets 3-5× the impressions of an equivalent text post, because each vote is a strong relevance signal back to the algorithm.
