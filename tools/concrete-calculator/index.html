Volume is length × width × thickness. Bag counts assume a typical pre-mix yield of roughly 0.45 litres of set concrete per kilogram, so they are an estimate; check your product, and order a little extra. For large pours, ready-mix delivery is usually cheaper than bags.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate concrete volume?

Multiply length by width by thickness, all in the same units. For a slab 3 m by 3 m and 10 cm thick that is 3 × 3 × 0.10 = 0.9 cubic metres. This tool converts the thickness from centimetres for you.

How many bags of concrete per cubic metre?

It depends on the bag, but a rough guide is about 110 × 20 kg bags, 88 × 25 kg bags, or 55 × 40 kg bags per cubic metre of set concrete. The exact yield is on the bag.

When should I order ready-mix instead?

Bags are convenient for small jobs, but mixing many by hand is hard work and inconsistent. For roughly half a cubic metre or more, a ready-mix delivery is usually easier, stronger and cheaper.
