Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Click rock, paper or scissors. The computer picks at random at the same moment, the winner is shown, and your running score of wins, losses and ties updates. Rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock.

Is the computer's choice really random?

Yes, it is chosen randomly each round with no memory of your previous moves, so there is no pattern to exploit and every round is fair.

Can I use it to make a decision?

Absolutely. Rock paper scissors is a classic fair way to settle a small dispute or choose between options when you cannot agree.
