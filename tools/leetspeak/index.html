Frequently asked questions

What is leetspeak?

Leetspeak (or 1337) replaces letters with similar-looking numbers and symbols, an internet subculture style from gaming and hacker forums. 'elite' becomes '31173'.

What is the difference between basic and full?

Basic only swaps the vowels a, e, i, o (so it stays readable). Full also swaps consonants like s to 5, t to 7 and b to 8 for the heavier classic look.
