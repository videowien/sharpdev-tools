Frequently asked questions

What is a tally counter used for?

Counting anything repetitive — people through a door, inventory, laps, reps, birds, drinks, or steps in a process — without losing track.

Will my count be saved if I close the tab?

Yes. The count is stored locally in your browser, so it's still there when you come back. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Can I count by more than one?

Yes — set the ‘Step’ field to any number and each press adds or subtracts that amount.
