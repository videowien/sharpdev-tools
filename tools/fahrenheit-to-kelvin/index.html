Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Kelvin?

Take the Fahrenheit temperature and subtract 32, multiply by 5/9, then add 273.15. The converter above does it instantly.

What is 1 Fahrenheit in Kelvin?

1 Fahrenheit equals 255.928 Kelvin. See the reference table for more common values like 0, 20, 37 and 100.
