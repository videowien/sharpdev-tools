Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a dead pixel and a stuck pixel?

A stuck pixel is permanently lit in one colour (red, green or blue) and often shows up on a black background. A dead pixel stays black and shows up best on white or bright colours. Cycling through solid colors helps you spot both.

How do I use this test?

Click Start test to fill the screen with a solid colour, then click to cycle through red, green, blue, white, black and more. Look closely (a magnifier helps) for any dot that does not match the rest of the screen.

Can this fix a stuck pixel?

Not directly, but rapidly flashing colors on the area sometimes helps a stuck pixel recover. A dead pixel (always black) usually cannot be fixed in software.

Does it use fullscreen?

It tries to go fullscreen so nothing is hidden behind browser bars. If your browser blocks that, the colour still fills the whole window.
