Frequently asked questions

How is my zodiac sign determined?

By the position of the Sun on your birth date in the western tropical zodiac — which maps to fixed date ranges, so only the month and day matter.

What if I'm born on a cusp?

Dates near a boundary can vary by a day depending on the year and time of birth. This tool uses the common date ranges; a full birth-time chart would refine edge cases.

What's the element of my sign?

Each sign belongs to Fire, Earth, Air or Water — shown above with your sign.
