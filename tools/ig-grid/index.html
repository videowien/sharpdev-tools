Why grid planning matters

People glance at your profile grid for ~3 seconds before deciding whether to follow. A grid with a clear visual rhythm — alternating light/dark, complementary colors, or recurring patterns — gives the impression of an "intentional" account.

Grid patterns that work

  • Checkerboard — alternate quote / photo / quote / photo. Easy to maintain
  • Color rows — three blue posts, then three red posts. Bold but committing
  • Photo + cover — every third post is a text-card; the other two are photos. Mixes content types cleanly
  • Carousel splits — a 3×3 image where each tile shows part of a bigger picture (see Carousel Splitter)

The "fold" matters

Only the top 6-9 tiles are visible above the fold on mobile. Front-load your grid with what you most want people to see right after they tap your profile.
