Common use cases

  • Outreach lists — pull all the handles from a Twitter / X thread or LinkedIn comment section
  • Hashtag audit — paste your last 30 captions and see which tags you actually use
  • UGC monitoring — paste a feed of user comments and pull mentions of your brand
  • Article link extraction — pull every URL out of a long form post
  • Comment moderation — surface every @mention to check for tag-spam

What gets recognised

  • Mentions — @ followed by letters, digits, underscore, period, hyphen (covers Twitter, Instagram, GitHub, Mastodon)
  • Hashtags — # followed by letters, digits, underscore. Stops at punctuation
  • URLs — http:// or https:// followed by a non-whitespace run, stops at trailing punctuation like . , )

Privacy

Text parsing runs entirely in your browser via regex. Nothing is sent to a server.
