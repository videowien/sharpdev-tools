What's safest for redaction

  • Solid black bar — strongest, irreversible
  • Heavy pixelate (20+ px) — visually obvious, practically irreversible
  • Light pixelate (under 10 px) — risky; AI face / plate recognition can sometimes reconstruct
  • Blur (any strength) — least secure; deconvolution algorithms can partially reverse it

When to use this

  • Screenshots with sensitive UI — emails, IDs, banking
  • Photos with bystanders — pixelate faces before posting
  • License plates in shared car / travel photos
  • Documents for examples — keep layout, redact actual data

Privacy
