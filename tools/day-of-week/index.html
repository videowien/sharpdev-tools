Frequently asked questions

How do I find what day I was born?

Pick your birth date above — the tool instantly shows the weekday, computed from the date with no time-zone surprises.

Does it work for dates far in the past or future?

Yes, for any date your browser's date picker accepts, using the standard proleptic Gregorian calendar.

What is the ‘day of the year’?

It's the position of the date within its year — January 1 is day 1, December 31 is day 365 (or 366 in a leap year).
