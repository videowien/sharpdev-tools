Twitter / X poll best practices

  • Two options outperform four — binary choices feel decisive and get more votes
  • Keep options short — even though you have 25 chars, 5-10 char options are visually cleaner
  • End with an "Other → reply" option when you want comments. People reply to disagree
  • 24-hour or 3-day duration hits the sweet spot — short enough to feel urgent, long enough to gather meaningful sample size
  • The question matters more than the options — frame as a real dilemma, not a leading question

What polls are good for

  • Audience research — what topic, format, or product variant resonates
  • Engagement bait (light) — polls boost reach because they keep users on Twitter longer
  • Settle internal debates — "should the button be left or right?"
  • Lazy content — polls take 30 seconds to write and trigger discussion in replies
