Common watermark patterns

  • DRAFT — large red, low opacity (10-20%), -45° rotation. Standard for documents in review.
  • CONFIDENTIAL — grey or red, slightly bolder opacity (25-30%). For internal-only material.
  • Copyright — small footer text like "© 2026 Your Company"
  • Sample / Preview — for sales documents you don't want copied verbatim

Watermarks aren't real security

A determined recipient can edit a PDF, screenshot pages, or print-then-rescan. Watermarks deter casual copying — they're not protection. For real document security, use PDF encryption (separate tool) and digital signatures.

Privacy
