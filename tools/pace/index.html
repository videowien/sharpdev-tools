Pace vs speed

Runners think in pace (time per distance, e.g. 5:00 min/km) — easier to gauge effort. Cyclists and drivers think in speed (distance per time, e.g. 12 km/h). They're inverse: pace = 60 ÷ speed.

Common training paces

  • Recovery jog — 6:30-7:30 min/km
  • Easy run — 5:30-6:30 min/km
  • Tempo / threshold — 4:30-5:00 min/km
  • 5K race pace (mid-pack) — 4:00-4:30 min/km
  • Marathon race pace (mid-pack) — 5:00-5:30 min/km
