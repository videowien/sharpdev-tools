Frequently asked questions

What is a CPS test?

A CPS (clicks per second) test measures how fast you can click a mouse button. You click as many times as you can within a fixed time, and the tool divides your clicks by the seconds to get your CPS score.

What is a good CPS score?

Most people score around 5 to 7 CPS. Above 8 is fast, and competitive clickers using techniques like jitter or butterfly clicking can exceed 10 to 12 CPS.

Does this work on mobile?

Yes. On a phone or tablet each tap counts as a click, so it doubles as a tap-speed test.

Is anything tracked?

No. The whole test runs in your browser and no clicks or scores are sent anywhere.
