Frequently asked questions

How do I add a border to a photo?

Choose an image, set the border width in pixels and pick a colour. The border is added around the outside, so the image keeps its full content, and the canvas grows to fit. Then download the PNG.

Can I make a white frame for Instagram?

Yes. A common look is a thick white border to fit a non-square photo into a square post. Set a generous width and white colour; for an exact square you can then run it through a resize or crop tool.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The border is drawn in your browser on a canvas and the image never leaves your device.
