Frequently asked questions

What does inverting colours do?

It replaces every colour with its opposite, so black becomes white, dark blue becomes light orange, and so on. The result looks like an old film negative.

What is it useful for?

Creating negative effects, previewing how a design looks in dark mode, improving the readability of some scanned documents, and artistic experiments.

Does it upload my image?

No. The inversion is computed pixel by pixel in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
