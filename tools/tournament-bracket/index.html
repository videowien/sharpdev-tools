Tournament Bracket Generator

Paste participants, get a randomly seeded single-elimination bracket with byes.

Frequently asked questions

How does the bracket handle odd numbers?

It rounds up to the next power of two and fills the empty slots with byes, so a player paired with a bye advances automatically to the next round.

Is the seeding random?

Yes, participants are shuffled with a cryptographic shuffle before being placed, so the matchups are randomly seeded each time you generate.

What bracket type is it?

Single elimination: lose once and you are out. Later rounds show as placeholders to fill in as results come in.
