Frequently asked questions

Is the ‘multiply by 7’ rule accurate?

No. Pets age fast in their first two years, then more slowly. This tool front-loads those early years and uses a gentler rate afterwards, which matches veterinary guidance far better.

Why does dog size matter?

Smaller dogs live longer and age more slowly later in life than large breeds, so the per-year rate is lower for small dogs and higher for large ones.

Is this exact?

It's a solid estimate. Real ageing varies by breed, health and individual, so treat it as a guide rather than a precise figure.
