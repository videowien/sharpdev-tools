Frequently asked questions

What are Stripe's standard fees?

For online card payments the common US rate is 2.9% of the charge plus a fixed $0.30. Rates differ by country, currency and product, so the fields here are editable.

How do I receive an exact amount after fees?

Switch to ‘I want to receive this much’. The calculator grosses the amount up so that after the percentage and fixed fee, your net equals your target.

Is this official?

No — it's an independent estimator. Always confirm the exact rate in your own Stripe dashboard.
