Frequently asked questions

What is a birthstone?

A birthstone is a gem traditionally associated with a month of birth. The modern list used here was largely standardised in 1912 and is the one most jewellers follow today.

What is a birth flower?

Like birthstones, each month has a traditional flower. They are popular for birthday bouquets and meaningful gifts, and this tool shows both the stone and the flower together.

Are there other birthstone lists?

Yes. Some months have alternative or older traditional stones, and a few cultures use different systems. This tool shows the common modern Western birthstone for each month.
