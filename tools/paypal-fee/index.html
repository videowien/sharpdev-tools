Frequently asked questions

How do I work out what to charge to receive an exact amount?

Switch Direction to 'I want to receive this'. The calculator solves the reverse formula, charge = (target + fixed) / (1 - percent), so after the fee you net exactly the amount you typed.

Are these the exact PayPal and Stripe fees?

They are common default rates. Real fees vary by country, currency, card type and account standing. Pick Custom and enter the rate from your provider's fee schedule for an exact result.

Does it store my numbers?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
