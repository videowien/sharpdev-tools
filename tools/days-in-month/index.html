Frequently asked questions

How many days are in each month?

January, March, May, July, August, October and December have 31 days. April, June, September and November have 30. February has 28, or 29 in a leap year. This tool shows it for any month and year.

How do I know if February has 29 days?

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except century years which must be divisible by 400. Pick February and a year and the tool tells you directly.

What are the first and last weekday for?

They are handy for planning, for example knowing a month starts on a Monday or ends on a weekend, when laying out calendars, rotas or pay periods.
