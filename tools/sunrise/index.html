Twilight phases explained

  • Civil twilight — sun 0 to 6° below horizon. Bright enough to do outdoor activities. Streetlights start turning on.
  • Nautical twilight — 6 to 12° below. Horizon still visible at sea (hence the name). The brightest stars become visible.
  • Astronomical twilight — 12 to 18° below. Sky appears fully dark to the eye; faint stars and deep-sky objects start being observable.

Use cases

  • Photography — "golden hour" = ~1h before sunset / after sunrise. "Blue hour" = civil twilight
  • Outdoor activities — when does it get dark enough to need a head torch
  • Astronomy — astronomical twilight is when serious observing starts
  • Travel planning — daylight hours at your destination during your visit
