PDF Form Flatten

Lock filled form values into static, non-editable text. Permanent.

Drop a filled PDF form here or click to browse

All form fields will be flattened — runs in your browser

What flattening locks

  • Text fields — typed values become permanent printed text
  • Checkboxes — checked state is locked in
  • Dropdowns — selected option becomes static text
  • Radio buttons — selected button becomes a marked circle
  • Digital signatures — appearance is preserved but signature is no longer validatable

When to use this

  • Sending a signed contract — recipient can't change your signed values
  • Submitting tax / legal forms — locks the answers
  • Form looks broken in recipient's PDF viewer — flattening fixes rendering
  • Need to print + the printer ignores form fields — flatten first

Privacy

The PDF is parsed and rewritten in your browser via pdf-lib. Nothing is uploaded.
