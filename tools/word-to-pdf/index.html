Word to PDF Converter

Convert .docx files to PDF directly in your browser. Nothing uploaded.

Drop a .docx file here or click to browse

.docx only — modern Word format (2007+). Your file never leaves the browser.

What gets preserved

  • Headings (H1-H6) with relative sizing
  • Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough inline formatting
  • Bulleted + numbered lists, including nested lists
  • Tables with cell content and basic borders
  • Hyperlinks as clickable PDF links
  • Inline images (embedded in the .docx)
  • Footnotes with reference markers

What may differ from the original

  • Fonts — your system fonts replace the original Word font
  • Page breaks — re-flowed based on content + margins
  • Headers / footers — partial support, may be dropped
  • Multi-column layouts — flattened to single column
  • Text boxes / shapes — converted to inline content where possible
  • Watermarks — not preserved (use the Watermark tool to re-add)

Why no upload?

Most "free" Word-to-PDF sites upload your document to their servers. That's a privacy and security problem — confidential contracts, NDAs, internal memos shouldn't leave your device. This tool converts in your browser using mammoth.js (DOCX parser) + html2pdf (PDF renderer). Your file never touches a server.

Privacy

The entire conversion happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
