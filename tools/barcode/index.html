Make Code128, EAN, UPC and Code39 barcodes. Download as PNG or SVG. Runs in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Which barcode formats are supported?

Code128 (the default, encodes any text), EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC, Code39, ITF-14, MSI and pharmacode. EAN and UPC need the exact digit count for their standard, otherwise the tool shows an error.

Can I download the barcode?

Yes, as a crisp SVG (vector, scales to any size) or as a PNG raster. Both render on a white background suitable for printing or embedding.

Is the barcode generated in my browser?

Yes. The data never leaves your device; the barcode is rendered locally with the JsBarcode library loaded from the Cloudflare CDN.
