About this toolkit

A set of photography calculators for shooting and planning: depth of field, hyperfocal distance, field of view, crop factor and equivalent focal length, exposure value, ND filter long exposures, flash guide number, time-lapse, the 500 rule for star photography, and print size versus resolution. Standard optics maths, all running in your browser.

Who uses these tools

  • Landscape & depth — Set the hyperfocal distance for front-to-back sharpness, and check the near and far limits of your depth of field before the shot.
  • Framing & gear — Compare lenses across sensors with crop factor and equivalent focal length, and see the exact angle of view and frame width at distance.
  • Exposure & light — Work out exposure value, the new shutter speed behind an ND filter, and the flash distance or aperture from a guide number.
  • Astro, time-lapse & print — Find the longest shutter before stars trail, plan a time-lapse end to end, and size a print at the right DPI.

Frequently asked questions

Are these photography calculators accurate?

They use the standard optics formulas: the hyperfocal and depth-of-field equations, angle of view from sensor size, the exposure-value definition, guide-number geometry and the 500 rule. Results are exact for the model; real lenses vary slightly with focus breathing and marked-versus-actual focal length.

Which sensor sizes are supported?

Full frame, APS-C (1.5x and Canon 1.6x), Micro 4/3 and 1-inch, with a custom crop factor where it applies. The depth-of-field and 500-rule tools use standard circle-of-confusion and crop values for each.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser with no download, sign-up, or data leaving your device.
