Frequently asked questions

How long can an Instagram caption be?

2200 characters maximum. Only the first ~125 characters show before the "more" cutoff — the Caption Counter shows you exactly where that falls.

Why doesn't Instagram preserve line breaks in my bio?

Instagram's bio editor strips standard line breaks. The Bio Formatter inserts invisible characters that Instagram's renderer treats as line breaks correctly.

What's the right size for an Instagram Reels cover?

1080×1920 pixels (9:16). But Instagram crops a centered 1080×1350 zone for the grid display — the Reels-Cover Sizer marks both zones so important content isn't cropped off.

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram?

The technical limit is 30 for posts and 10 for Reels (3-5 is the current recommendation for Reels reach). The Hashtag-Mix Optimizer balances small/medium/large-volume hashtags for best reach.
