About this toolkit

A focused toolkit for YouTube creators — download thumbnails, pull captions, extract video tags, count title/description characters, generate chapter timestamps and clean tracking URLs. Every tool here works directly with a YouTube video URL, no API key needed, nothing uploaded.

Who uses these tools

  • Thumbnail research — Download any video's thumbnail at max resolution to study what's working in your niche.
  • Caption extraction — Pull auto-generated or human-edited subtitles as SRT or TXT — useful for repurposing into blog posts, social clips or translations.
  • Tag & metadata audit — See exactly which tags a video uses, count title length against the 100-char limit, count description length against the 5000-char limit.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a YouTube API key to use these tools?

No. Tools that fetch data from YouTube (thumbnails, captions, tags) use public endpoints. Tools that work on text you provide (title counter, description counter, embed generator) just run in your browser.

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail in HD?

Open the Thumbnail Downloader, paste the video URL, and pick the resolution you want. Max resolution (1280×720 or higher) is offered when available.

Can I download subtitles as SRT and TXT?

Yes — the Subtitles tool returns both formats. SRT keeps timestamps for re-uploading; TXT is the plain text for blog posts and content reuse.

Are these YouTube tools free and is my data tracked?

Free, no sign-up. The site uses no cookies and only first-party privacy-friendly analytics — nothing is sent to YouTube on your behalf beyond fetching the public data you ask for.
